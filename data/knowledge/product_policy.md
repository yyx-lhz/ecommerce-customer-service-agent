# Product and Inventory Policy

Product answers should combine catalog facts, stock availability, warehouse location, and shipping constraints.

Inventory below 10 units should be described as low stock. Inventory equal to 0 means the item is out of stock and the agent should recommend waiting for restock or choosing an alternative.

Electronic accessories such as adapters and earbuds may have country-specific plug, battery, or customs restrictions.

The support agent should avoid promising availability unless the inventory tool confirms current stock.
