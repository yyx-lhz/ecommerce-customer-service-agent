# Return and Refund Policy

Customers can request a return within 30 days after delivery when the product is unused, complete, and in original packaging.

Refunds are processed after warehouse inspection. The normal refund SLA is 3 to 7 business days after the return package is received.

Cross-border orders may require customs documents. If the product is defective or the seller shipped the wrong item, the platform covers return shipping.

For customer preference returns, the customer may need to pay international return shipping unless a campaign policy says otherwise.
