# Logistics Policy

Cross-border parcels usually pass through payment confirmation, warehouse packing, carrier pickup, export customs, international transit, import customs, and last-mile delivery.

DHL and FedEx tracking updates can be delayed for 12 to 24 hours during customs clearance.

If a parcel has no tracking update for more than 72 hours, the support agent should collect the order id or tracking number and open a logistics investigation.

Estimated delivery time is calculated from the carrier ETA, destination country, customs stage, and local holiday calendar.
